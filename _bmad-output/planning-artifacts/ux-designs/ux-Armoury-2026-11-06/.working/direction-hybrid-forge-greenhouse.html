Greenhouse Instruments

The calm, ordered s.host room from Greenhouse, with one precise instrument at its heart: a hairline dial where all 3,847 pieces of software are still grey dots and only the three that need someone today glow ember. Underneath sits the full depth of the earlier operator console, with the same pages, graph, keyboard paths and exact identifiers, turned down to a quiet second layer so Tom gets an answer in a minute and Priya never loses precision.

What came from where

  • Greenhouse
    The shell: black sidebar with lime active item, white canvas, forest green selection and charts, three columns (nav / list grouped by state with counts / tabbed detail), thin rules, small uppercase buttons, lime primary.
  • Calm Instruments
    The dot-field dial (1 dot = 1 piece of software), bezel ticks, the 82% hairline arc, leader-line labels and small monospace captions. The same thin-line style draws the graph and the mini gauges.
  • Prior final design
    The full information architecture (renamed), the “Where it’s affected” graph, ⌘K and g h-style key hints, Forge ember as the one urgency colour, digests and CVE/KEV/EPSS as small grey detail, and Review in place of the slide-over drawer.

Ember is the only urgency colour. On white it is never text: glows use #D9A05B, dot cores and graph edges use #B8782A (3.7:1, above the 3:1 for graphics), words use #8A5410 (6.3:1). No red fills anywhere.
Lime means “act here” or “you are here”. Active nav, primary buttons, the coverage marker on the dial and the selected graph node. Forest means “looking at this”.
Precision is grey and monospace. CVE IDs, KEV/EPSS tags, digests and toolchain versions use text-3 #67716B at 10.5–12.5px (5.1:1 on white, 4.7:1 on paper).
