Wildcard direction · 05

Aurora Night

Security work often happens late and under pressure, so Armoury should feel like stepping outside on a clear, quiet night. Deep indigo sky, frosted-glass panels and moonlight text keep the eye relaxed. One soft aurora is the only colour flourish, and the three things that need you tonight appear as three gentle stars, not sirens.

How it differs from the four earlier directions

  • Dark, but soft: midnight indigo instead of a white canvas with a black sidebar. No lime, no forest green.
  • No sidebar: a floating glass pill bar across the top, so the whole width belongs to the content.
  • Glass, not lines: translucent panels with big radii and soft glows. No hard dividers.
  • An illustrated answer: the Home hero is a night sky where the 3 stars are the 3 things that need attention.
  • Warm urgency: amber, rose and periwinkle text and tints. No red anywhere.

The sky stays, the stars go

When nothing needs attention, the labelled stars leave and a crescent moon rises over still water. The aurora fades to a faint teal. There's no "0 issues" counter shouting at Tom; one sentence tells him he can close the tab.

The primary aurora button goes too, because there's nothing to do. The only action left is a quiet link back to this week's reviews, for anyone who wants reassurance.
