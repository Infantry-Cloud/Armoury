Armoury · UX direction

Field Notes

Security news, delivered like a calm weekly letter. Home is a short briefing with a headline that answers "are we safe?", three numbered things to look at, and a plain "What to do" line under each, so Tom is done in a minute. Priya gets the same clean pages with the exact IDs, tags and history in small grey type underneath.

How it departs from s.host

  • KeptThe black sidebar, the three-column master–detail, lists grouped by state with counts, the selected row in deep green, the tabs, thin rules, sparklines, the ring gauge and small uppercase buttons.
  • ChangedWhite becomes warm paper. A serif carries the headlines. Home reads top to bottom like an article, not a grid of cards.
  • LimeNot a big button fill. A highlighter pen: one marked phrase, the active item, a single dot on a chart.
