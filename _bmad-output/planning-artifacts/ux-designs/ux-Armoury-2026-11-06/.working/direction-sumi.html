Wildcard direction

SumiInk on paper.

Security news is loud, so Armoury should feel like a quiet page written by hand. Warm rice paper, brown-black ink, a lot of empty space, and an off-centre layout that reads top to bottom like a short letter: are we safe, what needs you, what can wait.

One rule does most of the work: vermilion only ever marks what needs you today, like a hanko seal on a document. If there’s no seal on the page, you can close the tab.

Light paper instead of a black sidebar Thin top bar, no side column Indigo + vermilion instead of lime + forest green Serif-led type, brush-drawn charts Two columns, set off-centre
