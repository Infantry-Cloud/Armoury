Armoury · Design direction

Still Water

Security is stressful, so the screen shouldn't be. Still Water keeps the s.host bones Tom and Priya liked — a dark rail, a list grouped by state, a calm detail pane — and warms them up: a deep green-black sidebar, warm paper and pale sage surfaces, rounder corners and more air. Home starts with one plain sentence instead of a wall of numbers, and a single soft ring and a low wave carry the "cool" without adding noise.

How it differs from the s.host reference

  • Sidebar is deep green-black (#0F1F1A), not pure black; the canvas is warm off-white and pale sage, not stark white.
  • Rounder and airier: 14–30px radii, inset rounded selection instead of a full-bleed bar, roughly 1.4× the padding.
  • A serif headline sentence leads Home; the ring and wave are soft, single-purpose visuals, not monitoring charts.
  • Deliberate deviation: buttons are mixed case ("Add software"), not uppercase, for warmth. Tabs are pills, not a square block.
  • Calm urgency: clay, ochre and slate tints with words and icons. No red fills anywhere.

The quiet state should feel like a reward.

When nothing needs attention, we take things away rather than add them. The list disappears, the nav badge goes, and the ring closes into a check with ripples spreading out, like a stone settling in still water.

Priya still has an easy way to go deeper: "See what we checked" opens the full record, and the 1,091 low-risk items are mentioned but don't ask for anything.

  • No red, no zero-state illustration clutter, one sentence.
  • Shows when we last checked and when we'll check next, so the calm can be trusted.
  • The lime bead and pine ring match the 82% ring on Home, now complete.
