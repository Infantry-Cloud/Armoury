Calm Console —
the operator console, made calm

The original console was the fastest way to an answer: key numbers in one band, a ranked list right underneath, a drawer that keeps you in the list, ⌘K and g-keys everywhere. Calm Console keeps that layout almost line for line and changes only how it feels.

The light canvas, black labelled sidebar, plain words and one quiet urgency colour (ember) come from the last hybrid. Its big dial, glows and three-column split are gone. Graphs stay where they help you decide: sparklines beside numbers, a small coverage dial, a hairline map of where something is affected.

Original mechanicHow Calm Console keeps it
Answer band at the top of Posturehero numeral + co-statsSame band, same place. Plain answer line above it (“3 things need attention today”), a sparkline beside each number, coverage shown as a 104px dial instead of a percentage.
Ranked findings table on the landing pageKEV first, then EPSS“Needs attention today” table sits directly under the band. The sort rule is written in words above every ranked table.
Full-width dense tables30px rows, mono identifiersStill full width. Comfortable rows are 42px, Compact rows are 28px (toggle in the top bar). IDs stay mono and grey, beside the plain name.
Triage drawer from any rowt · ⌘↵ record & advance · EscReview drawer docks on the right. The list narrows but stays readable and selectable. Same keys, same contents: why it ranks here, maker’s statement, decision, note, history.
⌘K search + g-key navigationpalette doubles as blast-radius query⌘K field in the slim top bar of every screen. The sidebar now shows labels with grey key hints (g h, g t …) so newcomers can learn the keys.
Blast radius graph ⇄ table4 tiers, running ring, caveat“Where it’s affected”: same 4 tiers as hairlines, one Graph / Table toggle, an inspector panel on the right (from the graph explorer), and the affected-software table underneath.
Coverage gaps as inline rows“unknown, not absent”Kept as a dotted, inline row at the position the missing data would occupy. Also listed on Home.
Pagination + freshness stripno infinite scroll“1–25 of 1,094 · page 1 of 44” with ‹ › buttons. A one-line feed freshness strip ends every screen.
