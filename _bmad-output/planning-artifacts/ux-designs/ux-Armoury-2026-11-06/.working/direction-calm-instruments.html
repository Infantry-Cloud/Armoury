Armoury · UX direction

Calm Instruments.

Security as a quiet, precise instrument rather than an alarm panel. The Home is built around one striking but restful visualisation: a watch-face dial where every one of the 3,847 pieces of software is a single dot. Almost all of them sit still and grey. Only the three that need someone today glow a soft amber, each with a thin leader line and a plain-language label.

Tom reads the sentence and the three glowing dots in under a minute. Priya gets exact counts, IDs and statuses in hairline type around the same dial. Thin rules, monospace captions and a single lime marker give it a Teenage-Engineering-meets-watch-dial feel without adding visual noise.

How it differs from the s.host referenceKeeps the black sidebar, lime active item, white canvas, grouped list with counts, deep-green selection, tabs and uppercase buttons. Swaps s.host's row of busy charts for one hero instrument (dot-field dial plus bezel ticks plus progress arc) and ring gauges drawn as hairlines, not solid fills. Red badges become muted amber and words. Every number has a sentence next to it.
