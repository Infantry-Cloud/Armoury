What changed from Greenhouse

The proposal to approve or reject. Each line says what changed and why.

Changes

Grouped by kind. Nothing here changes an agreed label, status, colour role or count.

Structure and hierarchy

  • Home separates the selected issue from fleet readings.The tabs belong to log4j-core, but “checked”, “open issues” and “audit report” are about all software. They now sit in their own band, “Across all software”, under the issue.
  • One lime action per view, everywhere.Home had two (Review all 3 and Plan the fix) and Software had two; every other action is now grey.
  • “Mark reviewed” and “Plan the fix” replaced by Review.“Mark reviewed” is not one of the five statuses, so every decision now goes through the two review questions.
  • Eyebrows and kickers removed.“Top of the list”, “Selected on the map”, “Vulnerability in a logging library”, “Needs attention · 0” and the tracked-caps “Deployed” label are gone, so each heading leads its own block.
  • One row shape in every list.Line 1: name and version. Line 2: severity · actively exploited · where. Right: the one column the list is about (places, issues or review status).
  • Review panel is shorter.The deployed list moved to the Exposure tab, so Save and next sits higher and the list fills to the pager (no gap above it).
  • Exposure shows its answer as one titled block.Query, view switch and actions on one line, then the answer in words. The inspector names the selection inline instead of with a kicker.

Words and counts

  • Software shows the real fleet.3,847 pieces of software: 1,126 deployed, 2,721 not deployed, with the 671 not checked yet as a coverage-gap row and a pager. The base said 6, which contradicted Home.
  • Issues counts reconcile with Home.Not reviewed yet 312 = Licences 1 · Critical 2 · High 39 · Medium 101 · Low 169. The base listed 4 unreviewed Critical items, which would make 5 need attention, not 3.
  • Issues summary says “3 need attention today”.Same words as the Home group and the badge (was “3 things to look at today”).
  • Licences get their own group.A licence has no severity, so AGPL-3.0 sits in a Licences group at the top. Licence issues are rare and each one needs a decision. When the count is 0, the group is hidden; the type filter still shows “Licences 0”. 1,085 vulnerabilities plus 9 licences make the 1,094 in the type filter.
  • Zero is scoped to what we checked.All clear now says it covers 3,176 of 3,847 (82%), so a quiet screen never suggests the whole fleet was checked.
  • Feed freshness is back on Home.A quiet strip names each source and its age (vulnerability data, exploited list, packages, clusters), as in the prior design.

Type and rhythm

  • Real typeface, embedded: IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono.Inter was declared but never embedded, so each machine showed a different system font. Plex is open licence, continues the prior spine’s Plex Mono rule and is not one of the overused UI faces.
  • Nine type sizes instead of twenty-two.12 · 13 · 14 · 15 · 17 · 20 · 24 · 30 · 40. Steps like 14.5 / 15 / 15.5 did no work.
  • Sentence case for table headers and graph tiers.Tracked capitals and mono read slower. Mono now marks only IDs, fingerprints, commands and keys.
  • Tabular numerals in all data.Counts line up in columns and do not shift when they change.

Browser surfaces and states

  • Themed selection, caret, focus ring and scrollbars.Lime text selection; forest focus ring on light, lime on dark, on the sidebar and on selected rows; thin scrollbars from the palette; native controls take the forest accent.
  • Hover, focus, pressed and disabled states.Rows, tabs, nav, segments, buttons, fields and pager. Save and next is disabled until a question is answered.
  • ⌘K is visible on every screen.A “Search or jump to… ⌘K” field heads the sidebar; g-keys show next to each page after pressing G.
  • Contrast fixed in both themes.text-3 darkened to pass on chips (4.9:1); backdrop captions now 5.5:1; the 10.5px KEV tag is 12px.

New screens

  • Reports, ⌘K palette, Checking, Registry unreachable.Prior-design functions that had no Greenhouse screen. No new report types.
  • Presentation without section numbers or lime caption keys.Lime belongs to the product’s active nav and primary action only.

What the critique found

  • Strong: it reads as a working product at a glance, and s.host’s calm survives real data.
  • Strong: the risk model is clean; severity, exploited and review status never mix.
  • Strong: Exposure answers in words first, then draws the map.
  • Fix: Home put fleet-wide gauges under one issue’s tabs.
  • Fix: counts disagreed between Home, Software and Issues.
  • Fix: two lime actions on Home and Software; “Mark reviewed” was not a status.
  • Fix: eyebrows over headings, tracked-caps labels, 22 text sizes, no embedded font.
  • Fix: no Reports, ⌘K, loading or error screen; no focus, hover or selection styling.
  • Fix: a gap above the Issues pager; a 10.5px KEV tag; captions on the backdrop at 4.1:1.

Detector, base: 64 findings: 29 low contrast, 13 cramped padding, 8 nested cards, 5 skipped headings, 3 numbered section labels, 2 undersized text, 1 eyebrow chip, 1 tight leading, 1 overused font, 1 kicker.
Detector, this file: 2 findings, both intended: 1 nested card (the ⌘K dialog drawn over a page) and 1 cramped padding (list rows run edge to edge so the forest selection fills the column). No low contrast, skipped headings, kickers, numbered labels, undersized text or overused font.

Kept as agreed

  • Greenhouse world, both themes, no-JS switch; lime only for active nav and one primary action.
  • Sidebar Home · Software · Issues 3 · Exposure · Reports · Settings · Help; title equals the label.
  • Severity groups with word and 4-step marker; no red fills; exploited first in each group.
  • “Actively exploited” quiet in rows, a pill in detail; KEV only in detail; no chance-of-attack rating.
  • Five review statuses, the two-question panel and its copy, word for word.
  • Deployed / Not deployed, Packages, Exposure graph and table, pagination, g-keys.
  • Counts: 1,094 open = 1,085 vulnerabilities (23 / 112 / 361 / 589) + 9 licences; 312 / 295 / 241 / 164 / 82; 3,847 software, 3,176 checked.
