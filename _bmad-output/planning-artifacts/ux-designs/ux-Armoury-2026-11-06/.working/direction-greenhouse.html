Armoury UX exploration · Direction 01

Greenhouse

Security work happens in a quiet, ordered room. Calm comes from emptiness and order: one black spine for wayfinding, a paper-white list that sorts everything into “Needs attention” and “Fine”, and a white detail canvas with very few numbers. Lime marks where you are and the one thing to do next; deep forest green shows what you’re looking at and draws every chart.

How it differs from the s.host reference

  • Keeps the shell almost as is. Same black sidebar, lime active bar, three columns, forest-green selection and tabs, small uppercase buttons.
  • The middle column sorts by need, not by kind. “Needs attention 3 / Fine 1,091” replaces “Active / Shutdown”, so Tom’s answer is in the first heading.
  • No red alert badge. The notification count is lime on black. Urgency uses muted clay and amber tags with words and icons, never filled red.
